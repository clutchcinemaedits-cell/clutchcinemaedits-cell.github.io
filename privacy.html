Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how the Clutch Cinema publishing tool ("the app") handles information. The app is a private tool run by the Clutch Cinema creator to publish Clutch Cinema's own videos to Clutch Cinema's own accounts on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X.

Information the app accesses

  • When the account owner connects a social media account, the platform issues access tokens that let the app upload and schedule videos to that account.
  • Basic account details returned by the platform (such as the channel or account name and ID) are used only to show which account is connected.
  • Post performance statistics (such as views) may be read for the owner's own content to decide which videos to make next.

How information is stored

Access tokens and account details are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in a self-hosted scheduling tool. They are not sent to any other server except the platform they belong to.

What the app does not do

  • It does not access accounts other than Clutch Cinema's own.
  • It does not sell, rent, or share data with third parties.
  • It does not collect information from viewers or the public.
  • It does not use data for advertising.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the platform's security settings, for example Google account permissions. Revoking access stops the app from posting, and stored tokens are deleted when the account is disconnected.
