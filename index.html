Clutch Cinema

Cinematic, beat-synced sports edits: NFL, NBA and soccer.

Clutch Cinema publishes short-form sports edits on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X.

About this app

The Clutch Cinema publishing tool is a private application operated by the Clutch Cinema creator. It is used only to upload and schedule Clutch Cinema's own videos to Clutch Cinema's own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
